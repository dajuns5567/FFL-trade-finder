## Source and method

Direct inspection of the canonical `value-history-data/value-history/index.json` branch and **16 consecutive immutable scheduled snapshot files** from 2026-11-12T03:51:18Z through 2026-11-14T21:29:10Z, each with 890–899 players; no snapshots were deleted or rewritten.

The archive index has 320 observations. Every examined scheduled snapshot has `valuation_contract: precision-idp-runtime-20260919`. That string does **not** prove all snapshots were generated by identical source-code revisions: the same label is reused over a changing period.

- Sleeper ID `7528`, RB, values over successive 2026-11-13 captures: **1330 → 1457 → 1193 → 1447 → 1451**, followed by **1208** early November 14. This is a real pattern in persisted source rows—not an SVG drawing error.

- Comparing 01:07 → 08:02 → 11:37 UTC, **nine players** reversed an absolute swing of at least 75 points and returned within 30% of the magnitude of their prior movement: IDs `12526, 13288, 9504, 13317, 13353, 8122, 12491, 6011, 11593`. Comparing 08:02 → 11:37 → 16:29, player `7528` had a 264-point fall and 254-point rebound.
- 2026-11-13 16:29 → 2026-11-14 03:09 UTC: **263 of 889 comparable players** moved by more than 80 points; **132 were IDPs**, consistent with a broad valuation or source-system adjustment rather than an isolated player-news event. The subsequent 03:09 → 06:33 snapshot had zero changes over 80 points.
- Every one of these records is marked `source: scheduled`. The retrieved archive alone cannot tell whether a particular shift came from dynasty publisher data, Sleeper production, a different valuation code revision, or a combination. **Do not claim proven publisher outage or invent stabilized player values from this evidence.**

## Guardrails staged in draft PR #465

1. On site page-load and manually requested snapshots, require **actual** `__fllConsensusRefresh.ok===true`, `successful>=7`, `__fllValueRefresh.phase==='complete'`, complete Sleeper history, and fully ready modeled player rankings. Previously the client accepted `complete===true` even when the refresh itself failed. A terminal unsuccessful refresh now leaves the archive alone rather than retrying and writing cached fallback as if it were fresh.
2. During any archival import, inspect the *two already-archived previous observations* before writing the next. When a cohort of at least six and at least 0.5% of comparable players shows a return-to-baseline swing of at least 75 points in each direction within 18 hours, **fail closed for that one snapshot batch**. New, non-reverting source/valuation changes are preserved. The next verified scheduled generation can retry automatically; no per-player manual values or smoothing.
3. The guard reports affected counts, sample Sleeper IDs, and exact rejected timestamp. The archive remains append-only, historical valuations and original trade records are never revalued.
4. The 96-combination Finder stress fix is **not** part of this History-only release; it remains separately isolated in PR #470. Combined draft PR #466 is test-only.

## Limits and acceptance

- This is a preventive source-integrity gate for **new** records, not a retroactive correction. The recorded 320 historical points remain untouched, including prior transient movements. Do not silently delete or replace them.
- Legitimate *cohort* reversals can occur with correlated news. The explicit fail-closed condition favors correctness over freshness in that exceptional case; if triggered, inspect the source evidence and rerun the next scheduled snapshot rather than waive the guard.
- A single-player uncorroborated spike may not trip the cohort gate. The source consensus completeness check and canonical parity checks still apply.
- Do not interpret reduced chart jaggedness as evidence the true dynasty value is unchanged: actual consensus developments should remain visible.
- No production deployment, merge or history rewrite is authorized.
