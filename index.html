Valuation model

Unified valuation model. Consensus is the dominant anchor; league scoring, Superflex, scarcity and IDP replacement level are filters, not permission to turn fringe players into premium assets.

  • Consensus market: dominant anchor; small league-specific adjustments.
  • Team fit: 0% of player value; partner selection only.
  • QB/RB scarcity: 15% • WR: 10% • TE: 2% • IDP: 0%.
  • TE is never treated as a lineup need.
  • Ordinary IDPs are not used as generic sweeteners. Elite IDPs can retain meaningful value.
  • Package values use diminishing returns so several weak assets cannot manufacture an elite asset.
